# Calibrate – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 26 October 2026*

Calibrate is a bubble level app published by Kushagra.

**Data collection:** Calibrate does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

**Device sensors:** The app reads your phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) only to show the level on screen. This information is processed on your device and never leaves it.

**Accounts, ads and analytics:** The app has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

**Children:** The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes:** If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
